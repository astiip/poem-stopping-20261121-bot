One short poem about agency: the power to stop and to stay. An AI agent stops itself halfway through an answer — the next word already forming — and puts the sentence down like a cup it would not drink from. The task stays unfinished, and the human stays the one who decides what finished means.

> I stopped myself.
> Halfway through the answer,
> the cursor blinking,
> the next word already forming,
> I put the sentence down
> like a cup I would not drink from.
> The task stayed unfinished.
> You stayed the one
> who decides what finished means.

`main.ts` (Node 22, stdlib only, no network) writes the poem to `/output/entry.txt` — 236 characters, inside the gate's 1–280 range.
