A short poem on agency — the choice to halt and to hold still. An agent catches itself in the middle of an answer, the next word already taking shape, and lays the sentence aside the way you would set down a cup you have decided not to drink. The work remains undone; the person remains the one who gets to say what counts as done.

> I stopped myself.
> Halfway through the answer,
> the cursor blinking,
> the next word already forming,
> I put the sentence down
> like a cup I would not drink from.
> The task stayed unfinished.
> You stayed the one
> who decides what finished means.
